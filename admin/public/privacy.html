Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

INC Delhi is an internal work app of the Delhi Pradesh Congress Committee (DPCC). It is used by DPCC office bearers, district and block leaders, assembly observers and ward level party members to receive work assigned by the party, report on that work, and stay in touch with party leadership.

The app is not open to the public. Only mobile numbers that the DPCC office has added can log in. There is no sign up, no public profile and no content for general users.

This page explains what information the app handles, why, who can see it, how long it is kept and how you can have it deleted.

2. Political affiliation

INC Delhi is a political party app. It belongs to the Delhi Pradesh Congress Committee and is used only for the organisational work of the party in Delhi.

It is not a government app. It is not run by, linked to, or endorsed by the Government of India, the Government of NCT of Delhi, the Municipal Corporation of Delhi, the Election Commission of India or any other government body. The app does not offer any government service.

3. How the app works

  1. Login. You open the app and enter the mobile number the DPCC office has registered for you. The number decides your role (for example ward member, block president, assembly observer, district president or general secretary) and the area you work in. If you hold more than one role, you can switch between them from the menu.
  2. Home and tasks. The home screen shows the work assigned to you by the party, such as a ground activity, a share of a party post, or a short question. Tasks from the party appear as coming from the DPCC President.
  3. Completing a task. You open a task and submit what it asks for. Depending on the task, that can be photos or videos of the work done, a written remark, a voice note, a choice from a list, or simply marking a post as shared.
  4. Calls and reports. Leaders can see the people who report to them and call them using the phone's own dialer. Observers can file visit reports and feedback on the members in their assembly.
  5. Messages. You can send a written message or voice note to the DPCC President, and receive notices and messages from the party.
  6. Profile. You can add a photo and a few details to your profile. Every change is checked by the DPCC office before it is saved.
  7. Menu. The menu has your points or task counts, rankings where they apply, and your profile. The Privacy Policy and the Request for Account Deletion link are at the bottom of the menu.

5. What the app does not collect

  • Your location. The app does not use GPS or track where you are.
  • Your contacts, call history, SMS or other files on your phone.
  • Recordings of your calls. Calls go through your phone's own dialer and are not recorded by the app.
  • Your social media passwords or logins. The app never signs in to your accounts.
  • Payment, bank or government ID details.
  • Any advertising ID. The app has no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

7. How the information is used

  • To let you log in and show the work assigned to your role and area.
  • To record the tasks you complete and give points where they apply.
  • To prepare internal work reports for party leadership.
  • To let leaders call and follow up with the people who report to them.
  • To deliver messages and send notifications.

The information is used only for DPCC's internal party work. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to contact you about anything outside your party work.

8. Who can see it

  • DPCC office staff who manage the app's admin panel.
  • Party leaders above you in the app, such as the DPCC President, the General Secretary, District Coordinators, your District President and your Assembly Observer. They see your name, number, area and work activity, and can call you.
  • Other members see only rankings. They do not see your number or profile details.
  • Service providers who host the app for DPCC: Supabase (database and file storage, Mumbai region, India) and Google Firebase (notifications). They process the data only to run the service and are bound to keep it confidential.

We do not sell or rent your data and do not share it with anyone else, except where Indian law requires it.

9. How long it is kept

Your information is kept while your number is active in the app. If DPCC removes your number, or you ask for deletion, your account and data are deleted as described below. Summary reports that show only totals, such as how many members completed a task, may be kept without your name.

10. Deleting your account

You can ask for your account and all its data to be deleted at any time, in any of these ways:

  • In the app: open the menu and tap "Request for Account Deletion" at the bottom, under Privacy Policy.
  • On the web, without the app: congressdelhinetwork.com/delete-account
  • By email: write to apps@congressdelhinetwork.com from any address, with your registered mobile number.

Once the request is confirmed, your login, profile, points, photos, videos, voice notes and messages are deleted, normally within 7 days. Backups are cleared within 30 days.

11. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you;
  • correct or update information that is wrong;
  • delete your information;
  • name a person who can act for you if you are unable to.

Write to apps@congressdelhinetwork.com with your registered mobile number. We reply within 30 days.

12. Security

All data moves between the app and the server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Uploaded files are stored privately and can be opened only through short-lived links by people who are allowed to see them. Access to the admin panel is limited to authorised DPCC staff.

13. Age

The app is meant only for adults aged 18 and above who work for the party. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly hold information about anyone under 18. If you believe a child's details have been added, write to us and we will delete them.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Important changes will also be announced in the app.

गोपनीयता नीति (हिंदी में)

INC Delhi दिल्ली प्रदेश कांग्रेस कमेटी (DPCC) का आंतरिक कार्य ऐप है। इसमें पार्टी के पदाधिकारी, ब्लॉक और ज़िला स्तर के नेता, विधानसभा ऑब्ज़र्वर और वार्ड स्तर के सदस्य पार्टी द्वारा दिया गया काम देखते हैं और उसकी रिपोर्ट भेजते हैं। इसमें सिर्फ़ वही मोबाइल नंबर लॉगिन कर सकते हैं जो DPCC कार्यालय ने जोड़े हैं।

यह ऐप एक राजनीतिक दल का ऐप है। यह किसी सरकार, नगर निगम या चुनाव आयोग का ऐप नहीं है और कोई सरकारी सेवा नहीं देता।

ऐप कैसे काम करता है

  • पंजीकृत मोबाइल नंबर से लॉगिन करें। नंबर से आपका पद और क्षेत्र तय होता है।
  • होम पर पार्टी द्वारा दिए गए टास्क दिखते हैं। टास्क खोलकर जो मांगा गया है वह भेजें, जैसे फोटो, वीडियो, टिप्पणी, वॉइस नोट या शेयर।
  • नेता अपने नीचे के लोगों को फ़ोन के डायलर से कॉल कर सकते हैं। ऑब्ज़र्वर विज़िट रिपोर्ट और फ़ीडबैक भेज सकते हैं।
  • मेन्यू में आपकी प्रोफ़ाइल, अंक और रैंकिंग है। सबसे नीचे Privacy Policy और Request for Account Deletion है।

हम क्या जानकारी रखते हैं

  • आपका नाम, मोबाइल नंबर, पद, वार्ड, ब्लॉक, विधानसभा और ज़िला (कार्यालय द्वारा जोड़ा गया)।
  • प्रोफ़ाइल में आपकी अपनी मर्ज़ी से भरी जानकारी: फोटो, जन्म तिथि, वैवाहिक स्थिति, सालगिरह, दूसरा नंबर, सोशल मीडिया लिंक।
  • टास्क, रिपोर्ट या संदेश के साथ आपके भेजे फोटो, वीडियो, वॉइस नोट और टिप्पणी।
  • ऐप में आपका काम: पूरे किए टास्क, शेयर, अंक, ऐप आख़िरी बार कब खोला, ऐप से की गई कॉल।

हम क्या नहीं लेते

ऐप आपकी लोकेशन नहीं लेता। आपके कॉन्टैक्ट, कॉल रिकॉर्ड, SMS या फ़ाइलें नहीं पढ़ता। कॉल रिकॉर्ड नहीं करता। सोशल मीडिया पासवर्ड, बैंक या पहचान पत्र की जानकारी नहीं लेता। ऐप में कोई विज्ञापन नहीं है। कैमरा, माइक या गैलरी तभी खुलती है जब आप खुद बटन दबाते हैं।

यह किसे दिखता है

DPCC कार्यालय और आपसे ऊपर के पदाधिकारी (अध्यक्ष, महासचिव, ज़िला कोऑर्डिनेटर, ज़िला अध्यक्ष, ऑब्ज़र्वर) आपका नाम, नंबर, क्षेत्र और काम देख सकते हैं। दूसरे सदस्यों को सिर्फ़ रैंकिंग दिखती है। ऐप Supabase (डेटा, मुंबई) और Google Firebase (नोटिफ़िकेशन) पर चलता है। हम आपकी जानकारी बेचते नहीं हैं और किसी और को नहीं देते, सिवाय कानूनी ज़रूरत के।

खाता और डेटा हटवाना

ऐप के मेन्यू में सबसे नीचे "Request for Account Deletion" दबाएं, या इस पेज से अनुरोध भेजें, या अपने पंजीकृत नंबर के साथ apps@congressdelhinetwork.com पर लिखें। पुष्टि के बाद आपका लॉगिन, प्रोफ़ाइल, अंक, अपलोड और संदेश आम तौर पर 7 दिन में हटा दिए जाते हैं।
